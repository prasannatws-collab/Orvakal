## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
**We do NOT collect, store, sell, or share any personal information.**
- No user registration, email sign-up, or phone number verification is required to use the app.
- No user profiles or personal records are stored on remote servers.

### B. Device Information & Diagnostics
- The application does not collect device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, advertising ID, Android ID).
- No third-party behavioral analytics, crashlytics, or user-tracking SDKs are embedded in the app.

### C. Permissions Hygiene
The application requests only minimal, necessary device permissions:
- **`android.permission.INTERNET`**: Used exclusively to fetch live agricultural mandi rates, check network connectivity, and open official external portals in the device's web browser when requested by the user.
- **NO Sensitive Permissions**:
  - The app does **NOT** request or use `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` or `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`.
  - The app does **NOT** request `CAMERA` or `RECORD_AUDIO`.
  - The app does **NOT** request `READ_CONTACTS` or `READ_CALL_LOG`.
  - The app does **NOT** use `CALL_PHONE`. All phone calls are initiated through the standard Android native dialer (`Intent.ACTION_DIAL`), ensuring full user visibility and manual confirmation before any call is placed.

## 3. Data Safety & Security

- **Encryption in Transit:** Any network communications initiated by the app (e.g., fetching mandi rates) are encrypted using standard industry-grade HTTPS/TLS protocols.
- **Data Retention & Deletion:** Because we do not collect personal data or maintain user accounts, there is no user data stored on our servers to retain or delete.
