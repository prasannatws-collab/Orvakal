2. Data Collection & Safety

🔒 Zero Personal Data Collection Guarantee We believe in complete privacy. The application does not collect, record, track, transmit, or monetize any personal user information.
  • No Account Required: Users can browse all village directories, industrial insights, schemes, and utilities without creating an account, logging in, or providing phone numbers.
  • No Location Tracking: The app does not request or track GPS/Location permissions (no ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION).
  • No Behavioral Tracking: No third-party ad networks, advertising identifiers, or tracking SDKs are installed.
  • Minimal Device Permissions: Only android.permission.INTERNET is used to load live agricultural prices and open public hyperlinks.
  • Dialer Hygiene: Clicking emergency numbers or business contacts opens the phone's native dialer (Intent.ACTION_DIAL). The app does not make calls automatically (no CALL_PHONE permission).

3. Security & Encryption

All communication with external servers occurs via HTTPS using Transport Layer Security (TLS), preventing tampering or eavesdropping.

4. Children's Privacy

The application is suitable for general audiences of all ages, including students and families. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13 years of age.
